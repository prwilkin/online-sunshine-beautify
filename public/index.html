Florida State Statute Cleaner

How to use

  1. Go to www.leg.state.fl.us and open any statute page.
  2. Copy the full URL from your browser.
  3. Paste it above and click Clean.
  4. The page will be reformatted with clearer indentation and collapsible sections.

Currently optimized for Florida Statutes. Other sites will not be fetched.
